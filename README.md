# AI for Street Safety Reporting

Client: City of San José, street safety reporting
Team: Arvin Vinluan; Sofia Amir; Ahmed Al-Tameemi; Nancy Kherella; Russell Jonathan Estrada, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I created the failure-case slide, showing how a high-risk road hazard could be misclassified and how human review and safety checks should address it.

## The problem

San José residents need a simple way to report street-safety problems such as speeding, potholes, fallen trees, and unsafe crossings. Without clear, structured reports and urgency indicators, city staff may struggle to quickly identify, prioritize, and respond to dangerous hazards.

## What we built

- A reporting prototype where residents select an issue type and identify its location using an address or map pin.
- A review and submission process where residents can add evidence, contact information, and details before submitting the report.
- The system organizes and summarizes reports, but city staff must still review urgency, confirm escalation, and decide what action to take.

## What I would do next

One gap we found is that the system could misclassify a high-risk report as routine, delaying emergency action. Next, I would build and test a stronger safety-check workflow that flags dangerous reports for immediate human review and provides residents with appropriate warnings.
